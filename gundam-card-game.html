THA GUNDAM TCG SIM

A tactical duel built on the real Gundam Card Game card pool. You choose one of seven 1st-place competitive decklists (GD04 Phantom Aria era); the AI takes a different one. You then roll for turn order, may mulligan your opening hand, and may redraw your Shields. Every card is genuine — real names, art, costs, AP/HP and effect text.

How to win (official): you defeat your opponent the moment a Unit deals them battle damage while they have no Shields left — so break all 6 🛡 Shields, then land one more hit. Running out of cards to draw (deck-out) is also an instant loss.

Turn phases (official order): 1 Start ready rested cards · 2 Draw draw 1 · 3 Resource place 1 from your 10-card Resource Deck · 4 Main deploy / pair / play / attack · 5 End.
The player going second gets an EX Resource (gold) for tempo. Each side also starts with 6 face-down Shields and an EX Base.

Playing a card costs two things: its Lv is a requirement — you must have that many total resources in your area — and its Cost is paid by resting that many active resources. A Lv 6 / Cost 4 Unit can't come down until you have 6 resources, even though you only pay 4. Cards show Cost (top-left) and L level (top-right).

Cards: Units fight with AP / HP. Pilots pair onto a Unit for an AP/HP boost — and if the pilot matches the Unit's Link, that Unit can attack the turn it's paired. (Play a Unit first, then tap the Pilot — it pairs automatically if you have just one Unit.) Commands are one-shot effects. Bases guard your Shields: while your Base stands, direct attacks hit it first.

Combat: attacking rests your Unit. An attacker may strike the enemy player or a rested enemy Unit — never an active one. Units trade AP simultaneously. A direct hit on the player goes to their Base first, then breaks one Shield; a broken shield is revealed and its 【Burst】 may trigger. With no shields left, the next hit wins.

Keywords modeled: <Blocker> intercept an attack · <First Strike> deal damage first when attacking · <High-Maneuver> can't be blocked · <Blitz> / Link — attack the turn deployed · <Repair N> heal N at end of turn · <Breach N> destroying a Unit breaks a shield · <Suppression> break 2 shields at once · <Support N> rest to boost an ally's AP · 【Burst】 shield triggers.

Controls: tap a hand card to inspect & play it; tap your active Unit then a rested enemy Unit or the enemy Shields to attack. When the AI attacks and you have a Blocker, you'll be asked whether to intercept.

Note: Resource Deck, EX Resource/Base, Shields, Burst, Bases, Blocking, First Strike, High-Maneuver, 【Deploy】/【When Paired】 effects and Link are all modeled, and both decks are built around Link pairs with an aggressive curve. Keywords are read as innate only (a card that merely "gains" one conditionally isn't treated as always having it). Each card's effect is still a best-effort reading of its printed text rather than a hand-coded version of every unique ability.
