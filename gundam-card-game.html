MOBILE SUIT CLASH

A two-player tactical card duel inspired by the Gundam Trading Card Game. Command the Earth Federation against the Zeon AI. Spend resources to deploy mobile suits, pair them with pilots, unleash command cards, and break through your opponent's shields.

How to win: Destroy all 6 of your opponent's 🛡 Shields, then land one more attack to win.

Each turn: 1. Draw 2. Gain +1 Resource (max 10) 3. Deploy / Attack 4. End Turn

Cards:
🤖 Units — Mobile suits with AP (attack) / HP. They have summoning sickness the turn deployed.
🎖 Pilots — Attach to one of your units to boost its AP/HP (Level-up).
⚡ Commands — One-shot effects: draw, damage, buffs, repairs.

Combat: Tap a unit to rest it and attack. The defender may block with an active unit (units trade AP as damage). Unblocked attacks destroy a shield, or — once shields are gone — win the game.
