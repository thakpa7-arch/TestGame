MOBILE SUIT CLASH

A tactical duel built on the real Gundam Card Game card pool. You command the Earth Federation (Blue / White) against the Zeon AI (Green / Red / Purple). Every card below is a genuine card — real names, art, costs, AP/HP and effect text.

How to win (official): you defeat your opponent the moment a Unit deals them battle damage while they have no Shields left — so break all 6 🛡 Shields, then land one more hit. Running out of cards to draw (deck-out) is also an instant loss.

Turn phases (official order): 1 Start ready rested cards · 2 Draw draw 1 · 3 Resource place 1 from your 10-card Resource Deck · 4 Main deploy / pair / play / attack · 5 End.
The player going second gets an EX Resource (gold) for tempo. Each side also starts with 6 face-down Shields and an EX Base.

Cards: Units fight with AP / HP. Pilots pair onto a Unit for an AP/HP boost — and if the pilot matches the Unit's Link, that Unit can attack the turn it's paired. Commands are one-shot effects. Bases guard your Shields: while your Base stands, direct attacks hit it first.

Combat: attacking rests your Unit. An attacker may strike the enemy player or a rested enemy Unit — never an active one. Units trade AP simultaneously. A direct hit on the player goes to their Base first, then breaks one Shield; a broken shield is revealed and its 【Burst】 may trigger. With no shields left, the next hit wins.

Keywords modeled: <Blocker> intercept an attack · <First Strike> deal damage first · <High-Maneuver> can't be blocked · <Blitz> / Link — attack the turn deployed · <Repair N> heal N at end of turn · 【Burst】 shield triggers.

Controls: tap a hand card to inspect & play it; tap your active Unit then a rested enemy Unit or the enemy Shields to attack. When the AI attacks and you have a Blocker, you'll be asked whether to intercept.

Note: Resource Deck, EX Resource/Base, Shields, Burst, Bases, Blocking, First Strike, High-Maneuver and Link are all modeled. Each Command's and Burst's effect is still a best-effort reading of its printed text rather than a hand-coded version of every unique card ability.
